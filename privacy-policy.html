LocPad Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026 · App: LocPad — GPS Route Simulator (Android) · Developer: TukimTK (com.tukimtk.locpad)
Summary: LocPad is a location simulation tool for software developers and QA engineers. We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. The application runs entirely on your device. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

LocPad does not collect or transmit to us (the developer): personal information (name, e-mail, phone number, account IDs); your simulated or real location — simulation data exists only in your device's memory; advertising or analytics identifiers; crash logs, usage statistics, or device fingerprints; or files on your device (GPX files are read only through the file you explicitly pick).

We operate no servers. We have no ability to see what you simulate.

2. Data Processed Entirely On Your Device

  • Simulated coordinates, speed, heading, and accuracy generated by the simulation.
  • Favorites and saved routes stored in a local database belonging to the app.
  • App settings (speed, base altitude, target application list) stored in local preferences.
  • The optional LAN Web Cockpit: when you start it, the app runs a short-lived web server on your device so a browser on the same local network can control the simulation. It is protected by a one-time pairing token, is reachable only from your local network, stores no session history, and stops when you stop it.

3. Network Requests Made by the App (Made by You, On Your Behalf)

The app contains no advertising or tracking SDKs. It makes outbound network requests only for the following user-initiated functions:

FunctionWhat is sentDestinationRetention
Map displayHTTP requests for map tiles (tile coordinates, not your position) OpenStreetMap tile serversGoverned by OpenStreetMap's own policy; the app does not attach your identity
Place searchA place-name query you type Nominatim (OpenStreetMap geocoder)Ephemeral; processed to return results
"Route to target"Origin and destination coordinates for route computation An Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM) endpoint, configurable by you; defaults to the public OSRM demo server Transient — coordinates exist in memory for the duration of the route computation and are not stored by the app

If you require strictly zero off-device location transmission, use a routing endpoint you host yourself (the endpoint is configurable in the app) or avoid the "Route to target" feature; all other functions work fully offline.

5. Children's Privacy

LocPad is a professional developer tool, is not directed at children, and is not intended for users under 18.

6. Third-Party Components

The app uses open-source libraries (Ktor, osmdroid, Leaflet, ZXing, AndroidX, Kotlin). None of them transmit your data. See the in-app "Community & advanced documentation" link and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES for the full list and licenses.

7. Your Rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to export, correct, or delete. Uninstalling the app removes all local data (favorites, routes, settings) permanently.
